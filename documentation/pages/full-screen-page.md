# Full-screen page

**Route:** OpenChamber sidebar → Extension pages → Design Chamber · **Access:** every OpenChamber user
**Purpose:** The place to work: browse every design as a card, pan and zoom a canvas wall of screens, pin comments to elements, review versions, and export a page as a ZIP or a single HTML file.

Screenshots: `documentation/pages/img/page-1400-<theme>-*.png` (1400 px wide, dark and light), generated by `test/ui/visual/shoot.mjs` — the real UI and guest core with the local service replaced by the in-memory fake in `test/ui/visual/`. The page-viewer shots (`*-page-*.jpg`, 1440 px) were taken from the same harness in OpenChamber's browser panel.

## What's on it

**Library (P1)** — [dark](img/page-1400-dark-1-library.png) · [light](img/page-1400-light-1-library.png)
- Toolbar: title, search, tabs **All / Canvases / Pages** (counts follow the search), **Sort** (Updated / Created / Title), **Settings**, **New ▾** (Canvas…, Page…).
- A responsive grid of cards (minimum 220 px): 16:10 thumbnail, title, kind, "updated" time, open-comment count and chat-link marks; pending designs say **Waiting for agent** on the thumbnail. The ⋯ menu is the same as the rail's.
- Cards update live from change events; scroll position is kept.
- Above the grid, the same notices as the rail: an earlier folder that still holds designs, and loose `.html` files in the designs folder offered for import as pages ([light](img/page-1280-light-1b-library-loose.jpg)). See the [rail panel](rail-panel.md) for the import flow.
- Empty: "No designs yet" ("Type /design or /page in any chat…") with **New canvas**.

**Canvas wall (P2)** — [wall](img/page-1400-dark-2-wall.png) · [hover](img/page-1400-dark-3-hover.png) · [composer](img/page-1400-dark-4-composer.png) · [pins](img/page-1400-dark-5-pins.png) · [pins zoomed out](img/page-1400-light-5b-pins-clustered.png) · [thread](img/page-1400-dark-6-thread.png) · [play](img/page-1400-dark-9-play.png)
- Toolbar: back to the library, the title (click to rename), **Edit / Play**, device-size chips (show or hide a column; at least one stays; remembered per design), zoom − % + **Fit**, **Comments · n**, **Versions**, **Continue in chat**, ⋯ (Export ZIP, Export screen as HTML, Reveal in folder, Copy path, Rename, Delete…).
- The wall is infinite in both directions. Every screen is a row, every device size a column; each artboard has a label — screen · device · size, "Home · Phone · 390×844" — that may run into the gap beside a narrow column, ends in … when there is still no room (the whole line is in its tooltip), carries the screen's ⋯ menu in Edit mode, and is hidden for rows below the first once the zoom leaves less than 18 px between rows; then a status: loading, "Not written yet", "N blocked" (hover for the list), "N remote", and a reset ↺ after you follow a link in Play mode.
- **Pan:** wheel or trackpad over the background, drag the background, Space + drag, middle-drag, arrow keys (Shift = bigger steps), **H** toggles the pan tool. **Zoom** (5–400 %): Ctrl/⌘ + wheel or pinch around the cursor, **+** / **−**, **Shift+0** for 100 %, **Shift+1** to fit everything, double-click a label to focus that artboard.
- **Edit:** hover outlines the element under the cursor; click it to open the comment composer (Add comment, ⌘/Ctrl+Enter; Cancel, Esc). Pins are numbered circles of one size at every zoom that follow the element and the artboard's scroll (pins scrolled out of the artboard hide); click one to edit, resolve or delete the comment, or press **Send this** ([card](img/page-1400-dark-ask-9-send-this.png)) to send only that comment to the chat now (an edited text is saved first; the card closes when it is sent; the other open comments stay open). A card that was opened before its comment went out another way, from the list, stops offering **Send this**, and a send that slips through is refused with a plain message instead of going twice. Zoomed out, pins that would overlap fold into one badge with their count (it looks like two stacked discs); click it to zoom in until they come apart. From 100 % up, pins on the very same spot sit side by side. A pin whose element is gone is dashed and listed under "Detached". The wheel over an artboard scrolls that artboard; hold Ctrl/⌘ to zoom or Space to pan.
- **Play:** the designs are clickable; links between screens navigate inside the artboard; a link to the internet asks "Open this link in your browser?". The **Pins** toggle shows the pins again.
- Changed files reload only the affected artboards; the camera and each artboard's scroll position stay. A design the agent creates and fills at once (the service reports only "added") reloads every artboard.

**Ask bar** — [with an element](img/page-1400-dark-ask-2-chip.png) · [a failed send](img/page-1400-dark-ask-5-error.png): a prompt under the wall, on a canvas and on a page (not under the drawers). Type what to change and press **Enter** (Shift+Enter adds a line, Ctrl/⌘+Enter also sends); **Send** is the ask bar's main button (filled, and dimmed while the box is empty). The message goes where a comment batch goes: the design's linked chat when it is the selected one, otherwise the linked chat is opened and the message is delivered there, or a new linked chat starts when there is none or it is gone. A small line under the box says which ("Goes to “Coffee subscription chat”", "Goes to the linked chat", "Starts a new chat"). If the agent is busy, the message is added to the end of what is already in the chat box instead of replacing it, and a toast says so. Clicking an element in Edit (or Comment on a page) opens the comment card and also attaches that element to the bar as a chip (tag, a few words of its text, and where it is); the agent then gets the screen, device size and element with your words. A new click replaces the chip; adding the comment removes it (the element has a comment now); cancelling the comment keeps it; **×**, Esc in the bar, a sent message, Play and an old version on show remove it. Removing the chip, or sending, never throws away a comment you have started typing in the open card: the card stays. Esc with no chip hands the keyboard back to the wall. The box grows to six lines, then scrolls; from 10 000 characters it shows a counter, and over 12 000 says by how many to cut; a message is never shortened for you. While it sends the box is read only and Send spins; a failure keeps your text and the chip and says what went wrong under the box. While an old version is on show, asking is off ("Go back to the current design to ask the agent."). Asks are not saved anywhere but the chat.

**Build strip (P2, P6)**: [working](img/page-1400-dark-build-1-working.png) · [writing](img/page-1400-dark-build-2-writing.png) · [question](img/page-1400-dark-build-3-question.png) · [done](img/page-1400-dark-build-4-done.png) · [retrying](img/page-1400-dark-build-5-retrying.png) · [failed](img/page-1400-dark-build-6-failed.png) · [nothing written yet](img/page-1400-dark-build-7-stalled.png)
- While the agent that is linked to a design works on it (after `/design`, `/page`, Send comments to chat or Continue in chat), a small pill floats at the top centre of the wall and says what is going on. It sits over the wall and never moves it; when there is nothing to say it is not there. It follows two things the page already knows: the files the agent saves into the design, and the activity of the linked chat.
- What it says, first match wins: **Reconnecting to the local service** (the service has been unreachable for 5 seconds, only if the pill was already showing) · **The agent is asking you something in the chat** and **The agent needs your OK in the chat** (**Open chat**) · **The agent stopped with an error** (**Open chat**, **Dismiss**) · **Writing screens/pricing.html** with "2 of 4 screens written" on a canvas (for 15 seconds after a file lands; not shown while the linked chat is idle, so saving a file yourself does not look like the agent at work) · **The agent hit a problem and is retrying** · **The agent is working**, with "Last saved pricing.html 1 min. ago" once a file landed, or "Nothing saved in the last 2 minutes" · **Done. Updated 3 files** (or "Done. No files changed" with **Open chat**), which stays for 8 seconds · **Nothing written yet. Check the chat for a question.** (a design with no file 3 minutes after it was created; **Open chat**, **Dismiss**).
- While the agent works, writes or retries, a running time ("0:42") follows the text. A dot in the status colour comes first; the wording always says the same thing, so colour is never the only cue. The dot pulses slowly while the agent is busy (not with reduced motion).
- A restore you make from Versions is not the agent: the files it rewrites, and the temp files the service writes on the way, show nothing and light no artboard. If the host briefly stops reporting the chat's activity (a reconnect), the pill keeps showing where things were for up to 30 seconds instead of starting over.
- It never claims "Done" or an error for a turn it did not see start: opening a design whose chat is idle shows nothing. Without a linked chat (or without permission to read chats) only the file-based states show. **Dismiss** hides an error or the "nothing written" notice until the next turn starts or a new file lands. **Open chat** is Continue in chat, so on this page it closes the page.
- The artboard the agent just wrote gets an accent ring that fades over 6 seconds. Tokens and asset files, and pages (one artboard), light nothing.
- The pill is a status region (`role="status"`, polite): a change of phase or file is spoken once, the running time is not.

**Comments drawer (P3)** — [open](img/page-1400-dark-7-comments.png) · [sent](img/page-1400-dark-7b-comments-sent.png): beside the wall, never over it — except on a page narrower than 720 px (beside an open rail), where the drawers lie over the wall's right half and **Fit** fills the part left uncovered (the whole wall when less than 240 px is left). Tabs Open / Sent / Resolved with counts, comments grouped by screen with their pin number, device size and element; click one to jump to it; ✓ resolves. The footer button says what it does — **Send 3 comments to chat** — and when it cannot, says why. Sending makes one revision request that also says which pinned elements moved or are missing; it goes to the linked chat (or, when that chat isn't selected, opens it and is delivered there; or starts a new chat). If the agent is busy it waits in the chat box. If the chat got the message but the comments could not be marked as sent, a notice says to resolve them by hand (they are never reported as a failed send).

**Versions drawer (P4)** — [compare](img/page-1400-dark-8-versions-compare.png): newest first with the reason ("After agent turn", "Auto-saved", "Saved manually", "Before restore", "Restored"), a **Current** mark or an **Unsaved changes** row. **View** shows that version on the wall (read only, with a bar: Compare with current · Restore · Back to current); **Compare** splits each artboard — the version on the left, the current design on the right — and lists the changed files; **Restore** restores at once, with no confirmation: the "Viewing" bar goes and a notice under the toolbar says "Restored v4. Your previous state is saved as v6." with **Undo** ([notice](img/find-restore-page-1400-light-2-restored.png)). It stays 10 seconds and waits while the pointer or focus is on it. **Undo** restores the state that was replaced ("Back to v6. v4 is still in Versions.", 5 seconds, no Undo of the Undo). If Undo fails the notice stays with **Try again** and **Dismiss**. A failed restore is a toast that says nothing was changed. While one restore runs, every Restore button (the list and the Viewing bar) is disabled. When the version list had to be repaired, a banner sits above the list: "Some versions could not be read", "Version list restored from its backup" or "Older versions could not be recovered" (about 30 days). A list saved by a newer Design Chamber shows "Saved by a newer Design Chamber" and nothing else: no Save button, no empty state.

**Settings (P5)** — [sheet](img/page-1400-dark-13-settings.png): the same content as the [rail's settings](rail-panel.md) (folder, earlier folders, help your agent find your designs, browser, opencode.json), in a side sheet.

**Page viewer (P6)** — [full width](img/page-1440-dark-14-page-full-width.jpg) · [laptop view with pins](img/page-1440-dark-15-page-laptop-pins.jpg) · [export menu](img/page-1400-dark-11-artifact-export-menu.png) · [exported](img/page-1400-dark-12-artifact-exported.png)
- A page is shown like a browser tab, not on a wall: there is no pan and no zoom, and no zoom controls. Toolbar: back, title, **View**, **Comment**, then **Comments · n**, **Versions**, **Export ▾** (Download ZIP, Download single-file HTML), **Copy ▾** (Copy HTML when it fits the clipboard limit, Copy path), **Continue in chat**, ⋯.
- **View:** **Full width** (the default) fills the viewer and the page scrolls inside its own frame. Each design size ("Laptop · 1280×800") shows the page at that width, centred, as tall as the viewer, shrunk to fit when the viewer is narrower and never enlarged. The choice is remembered per design; a size the agent removes falls back to Full width.
- The page is interactive like a browser. **Comment** turns on comment mode: hover outlines elements, a click opens the composer. Pins work in every view, Full width included: every comment on the page shows in every view, re-found in the current layout, and a new comment remembers the view it was made in ("Made at full width" in its card). Pins that would overlap sit side by side.
- Wheel or trackpad over the page scrolls it; arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Home and End scroll it when the viewer has focus. Ctrl/⌘ + wheel, **+ / −**, **Shift+0 / Shift+1**, Space-drag and **H** do nothing here.
- **Remote files:** when the page loads files from the internet, a warning says "This page loads N files from the internet" and "They're blocked here because designs run offline:", then lists up to 5 of them by host and path (the full address on hover) and "+ n more". **Ask the agent to fix it** asks the service for an offline request that lists every blocked address and delivers it to the linked chat as one message, the way comments do (opening that chat, or starting a new one when there is none or it was deleted; if the agent is busy it waits in the message box). The button spins while that runs; a failure shows the service's sentence. **Hide** puts the banner away for that design until OpenChamber restarts or the extension reloads (closing and reopening the design keeps it hidden); it comes back if a file it didn't list gets blocked, and stays away if some of the listed ones are fixed. The comments drawer note and the revision's URL list are not affected. The same banner appears on a canvas ("This design loads…"), whose artboards also keep their "N remote" badges. With blocked files, the comments drawer says "The blocked files are listed in the request too." The banner is hidden while you look at an old version.
- After an export the toast carries the path and Copy, and a banner offers **Show in folder**.

**Checks badge (P7)** — [issues](img/checks-page-1400-dark-1-badge-issues.png) · [findings](img/checks-page-1400-dark-2-findings.png) · [after a rendering check, light](img/checks-page-1400-light-5-after-run.png) · [passed](img/checks-page-1400-light-7-pass.png) · [never rendered](img/checks-page-1400-dark-9-never-checked.png) · [no browser](img/checks-page-1400-light-10-no-browser.png) · [didn't load](img/checks-page-1400-dark-12-error.png)
- A pill at the right end of the header on both a canvas and a page, just before **Comments**: **Checking**, **No issues**, **1 issue** / **3 issues** (errors plus warnings; notes never count) or **Checks** when the service did not answer. It is red with errors, amber with only warnings, green for a pass and grey while unknown, and it always carries words and a shape, never colour alone. Its accessible name is "Checks: 3 issues". It is hidden while you look at an old version, because the checks describe the files as they are now.
- A pass is green only when nothing more can be learned: the rendering was checked on the current files, or no browser exists to check it with. Clean files whose rendering was never checked (or was checked before your latest changes) show a grey **No issues**, and its description says what is missing.
- It opens a popover under the badge (up to 420 px wide, 60 % of the window tall, scrolls). Focus goes to **Ask the agent to fix**, Tab stays inside, Escape closes it and puts focus back on the badge, and a click outside closes it. The popover shows, top to bottom: a one-line summary ("2 errors, 1 warning. 1 note."); the rendering line ("Rendered 6 artboards 4 min. ago.", "Rendering hasn't been checked yet.", "Rendering was checked before your latest changes.", "Checking the rendering…", "No Chrome, Edge or Chromium was found, so only the files were checked.", "The last rendering check failed."); the findings in groups **Needs fixing** (errors), **Worth a look** (warnings), **Notes** and **From before your latest changes** (the last two start closed; the old group is grey and not counted); then the two buttons.
- Each finding is the service's sentence ("screens/home.html @ Phone 390×844: the content is 452px wide, wider than the 390px viewport.") with the selector or text snippet from the design under it, in a code font, one line, full value on hover. Design text is only ever shown as text.
- The filled button is the next step: **Ask the agent to fix** when something is counted, **Check rendering** when nothing is (the other one is an outline button). **Ask the agent to fix** sends the linked chat one message listing the errors and warnings, the same way comments are sent. If that chat is open and idle, the message is sent and a toast says "Sent to the chat." If the agent is busy, the message waits in the chat's message box. If another chat is open, Design Chamber opens the linked one to send it ("Opening the linked chat to send it."). If the design has no chat or its chat was deleted, a new chat starts with the request ("Started a new chat with the request."). The popover closes in every case. It is off, with the reason in the summary above it, when nothing is counted. A rendering made before your latest edit is not sent. **Check rendering** (**Check again** once it has run) renders every screen at every size in the background; the badge reads **Checking**, a screen reader hears "Checking the rendering…" once and "Rendering check finished. 3 issues." once (one live region on the badge; the rendering line in the popover is not live), and the new findings are counted. It is hidden when no browser is found and no check is running; if another design is being checked, a toast says to try again in a moment.
- States: loading is a skeleton in the popover and **Checking** on the badge; a failed load says "Checks didn't load" with the service's sentence and **Try again** (the badge reads **Checks**); a failed refresh keeps the last answer on screen with "Checks didn't refresh". The badge refreshes within a second of a change to the design's files, `design.json` or check results, and while a check runs it also asks every 4 seconds in case the change feed is down.

**Edit text (P2, P6)**: pick an element in Edit mode (canvas) or Comment mode (page) and a toolbar floats above it with **Edit text**, **Comment** and **Ask agent**, next to the comment box. **Edit text** (or a double-click on the text) opens a small card over the element, sized to it (260 to 480 px); Enter saves, Esc discards, a click away with a change keeps it open with a reminder, and a saved edit shows "Text updated in <title>" with **Undo** for 10 seconds (Ctrl or ⌘+Z undoes the last one once). When an element can't be edited the button is dimmed and one sentence says why. Full description, every refusal and the keyboard path: [Edit on the canvas](../features/edit-on-canvas.md#editing-text-what-the-user-sees-full-screen-and-rail). Screenshots come from `test/ui/visual/scenarios/edit-text.mjs` (`node test/ui/visual/shoot.mjs edit-text --out documentation/pages/img`).

**Screen controls (P2)**: on a canvas in Edit mode every screen row has a ⋯ button at the end of its first label, and a dashed **+ Screen** board sits after the last row. The ⋯ menu offers **Duplicate**, **Move up**, **Move down**, **Move to trash** and **Try 3 directions…**; an entry that cannot run says why inside itself ("This is the last screen."), and the arrow keys still reach it. **Drag** a row's first label to put the screen in a new place (a chip follows the pointer, a line shows where it lands, Esc cancels). Each change shows a notice with **Undo**. Comments on a trashed screen stay in the Comments drawer under "<id> · Screen removed". **+ Screen** opens a box "What should the new screen show?" that sends the request to the linked chat; the words are kept if it fails. Hidden in Play mode and on an old version; at 40 screens the board goes and the menu says "A canvas holds up to 40 screens." Full description: [Screens and directions](../features/screens-and-directions.md#on-the-wall-what-the-user-sees). Screenshots come from `test/ui/visual/scenarios/screens.mjs` (`node test/ui/visual/shoot.mjs screens --out documentation/pages/img`).

**Tweaks (P2, P6)**: when a design lists knobs in design.json (accent hue, corner radius, density…), a **Tweaks** button sits between **Comments** and **Versions**; a small dot on it means there are values you have not saved. It opens a 360 px panel beside the wall (the same Comments/Versions slot: opening one closes the other). Each knob is a label with its value and unit ("Corner radius, 8 px") over a slider, or over a segmented choice for named options; the arrow keys step a slider, **Page Up / Page Down** move ten steps, **Home / End** go to the ends. Moving a knob changes every artboard at once, without a reload, with the scroll, Play state and camera kept; nothing is written. "Unsaved" shows next to the title and **Send** in the ask bar drops to an outline so **Save** is the one filled button on screen. **Save** writes the values to `tokens.css` and a notice reads "Tweaks saved to tokens.css" with **Undo**; **Reset** returns to the file's values and writes nothing (both are off, with the reason in the foot, until there is something to save). A knob that cannot be tuned (a formula, another unit, no such variable) is dimmed with its reason under it; a variable another rule also sets says "Also set for @media (max-width: 700px); only the default changes." and that the preview shows the value everywhere. If `tokens.css` cannot be read the panel says "Couldn't read tokens.css. <why>" with **Try again**, the knobs and buttons are off and your unsaved values are kept. If the agent rewrites `tokens.css` under your values a warning offers **Apply to the new file** or **Discard my tweaks** (Save is off until you choose). While the agent works on the design the foot says "The agent is working on this design. It may write over your edit." Unsaved values survive closing the panel and a reload of the extension frame; leaving the design with unsaved values asks **Save your tweaks?** (**Keep editing**, **Discard**, **Save**). On an old version the panel is read only ("You're viewing an old version."). A design with no knobs has **Ask for tweaks** in the ⋯ menu: it puts "Add tweaks for this design (accent hue, density, type scale, radius)." in the ask bar and sends nothing. ## Behaviour by role
One role: the OpenChamber user. "Continue in chat" reopens the linked chat, or starts a new one. A design chip or "Open as page" that targets this page opens that design.

**Keeps your place.** The page's frame reloads whenever the extension updates, which used to drop you back on the library. Opening a design now stores its slug in `host.storage` under `place:page` and going back to the library clears it; on start the page reopens that design if it is still in the list (a design that is gone, or a design you open before the page has finished starting, falls back to the library or wins, quietly). Same code and rules as the [rail](rail-panel.md#keeps-your-place); the rail has its own key, `place:panel`.

## Data
**Reads:** as the [rail](rail-panel.md), plus the design file for an Edit text check (`GET /designs/:slug/source`, kept for a moment per screen and dropped when the file changes), renders of each screen/viewport and version (`GET /designs/:slug/render`). The build strip also reads the change feed and, from the host, the projects and the sessions of the design's project (`listProjects`, `onSessions`; read only).
**Writes:** a text edit (`POST /designs/:slug/edits`, `op: 'text'`, only from Save in the editor), the screen actions (`op: 'reorder' | 'duplicate' | 'trash-screen'`, only from a menu entry or a drop) and their Undo (`POST /designs/:slug/edits/:id/undo`); comments, rename, delete (to `.trash`), versions (snapshot, restore), exports (to Downloads), settings, loose-file import (`POST /loose/import`). Per-design view settings (hidden sizes and camera for a canvas, `pageView` for a page) are saved with `host.storage` under `view:<slug>`; the open design under `place:page`. A comment made on a page records its view in `viewport` (`fill` for Full width). "Ask the agent to fix it" calls `POST /designs/:slug/prompt` with `purpose: 'offline'`. The ask bar calls it with `purpose: 'ask'` (the words and an optional element); one comment is sent with `purpose: 'revision'` and that comment's id. The Checks badge reads `GET /designs/:slug/checks`, starts a rendering check with `POST /designs/:slug/checks/run` and asks the agent with `POST /designs/:slug/prompt` and `purpose: 'checks'`.

## States & edge cases
- Every data view has loading (skeletons / scoped spinners), empty (says what goes here and how to add it), error (the service's sentence + Try again, input kept) and success states.
- A design deleted while open shows "This design is gone" with a way back; a pending design shows "isn't written yet" boards that fill in live ([screenshot](img/page-1400-dark-live-1-not-written.png)).
- Remote assets are blocked by the host; the artboard's "N remote" badge and the remote-files banner say so. When nothing is blocked any more, "Ask the agent to fix it" says "This design doesn't load anything from the internet."
- A page's frame follows the viewer's size (resizes settle for 100 ms) without reloading; a drawer laid over a narrow page makes the frame narrower instead of covering it.
- The page never puts agent-written HTML in its own DOM: every artboard is a sandboxed frame (`allow-scripts`, never `allow-same-origin`).
