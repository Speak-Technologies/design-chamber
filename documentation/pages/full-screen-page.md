# Full-screen page

**Route:** OpenChamber sidebar → Extension pages → Design Chamber · **Access:** every OpenChamber user
**Purpose:** The place to work: browse every design as a card, pan and zoom a canvas wall of screens, pin comments to elements, review versions, and export a page as a ZIP or a single HTML file.

Screenshots: `documentation/pages/img/page-1400-<theme>-*.png` (1400 px wide, dark and light), generated by `test/ui/visual/shoot.mjs` — the real UI and guest core with the local service replaced by the in-memory fake in `test/ui/visual/`. The page-viewer shots (`*-page-*.jpg`, 1440 px) were taken from the same harness in OpenChamber's browser panel.

## What's on it

**Library (P1)** — [dark](img/page-1400-dark-1-library.png) · [light](img/page-1400-light-1-library.png)
- Toolbar: title, search, tabs **All / Canvases / Pages** (counts follow the search), **Sort** (Updated / Created / Title), **Settings**, **New ▾** (Canvas…, Page…).
- A responsive grid of cards (minimum 220 px): 16:10 thumbnail, title, kind, "updated" time, open-comment count and chat-link marks; pending designs say **Waiting for agent** on the thumbnail. The ⋯ menu is the same as the rail's.
- Cards update live from change events; scroll position is kept.
- Above the grid, the same notices as the rail: an earlier folder that still holds designs, and loose `.html` files in the designs folder offered for import as pages ([light](img/page-1280-light-1b-library-loose.jpg)). See the [rail panel](rail-panel.md) for the import flow.
- Empty: "No designs yet" ("Type /design or /page in any chat…") with **New canvas**.

**Canvas wall (P2)** — [wall](img/page-1400-dark-2-wall.png) · [hover](img/page-1400-dark-3-hover.png) · [composer](img/page-1400-dark-4-composer.png) · [pins](img/page-1400-dark-5-pins.png) · [pins zoomed out](img/page-1400-light-5b-pins-clustered.png) · [thread](img/page-1400-dark-6-thread.png) · [play](img/page-1400-dark-9-play.png)
- Toolbar: back to the library, the title (click to rename), **Edit / Play**, device-size chips (show or hide a column; at least one stays; remembered per design), zoom − % + **Fit**, **Comments · n**, **Versions**, **Continue in chat**, ⋯ (Export ZIP, Export screen as HTML, Reveal in folder, Copy path, Rename, Delete…).
- The wall is infinite in both directions. Every screen is a row, every device size a column; each artboard has a label — screen · device · size, "Home · Phone · 390×844" — that may run into the gap beside a narrow column, ends in … when there is still no room (the whole line is in its tooltip), and is hidden for rows below the first once the zoom leaves no room between rows; then a status: loading, "Not written yet", "N blocked" (hover for the list), "N remote", and a reset ↺ after you follow a link in Play mode.
- **Pan:** wheel or trackpad over the background, drag the background, Space + drag, middle-drag, arrow keys (Shift = bigger steps), **H** toggles the pan tool. **Zoom** (5–400 %): Ctrl/⌘ + wheel or pinch around the cursor, **+** / **−**, **Shift+0** for 100 %, **Shift+1** to fit everything, double-click a label to focus that artboard.
- **Edit:** hover outlines the element under the cursor; click it to open the comment composer (Add comment, ⌘/Ctrl+Enter; Cancel, Esc). Pins are numbered circles of one size at every zoom that follow the element and the artboard's scroll (pins scrolled out of the artboard hide); click one to edit, resolve or delete the comment. Zoomed out, pins that would overlap fold into one badge with their count (it looks like two stacked discs); click it to zoom in until they come apart. From 100 % up, pins on the very same spot sit side by side. A pin whose element is gone is dashed and listed under "Detached". The wheel over an artboard scrolls that artboard; hold Ctrl/⌘ to zoom or Space to pan.
- **Play:** the designs are clickable; links between screens navigate inside the artboard; a link to the internet asks "Open this link in your browser?". The **Pins** toggle shows the pins again.
- Changed files reload only the affected artboards; the camera and each artboard's scroll position stay. A design the agent creates and fills at once (the service reports only "added") reloads every artboard.

**Comments drawer (P3)** — [open](img/page-1400-dark-7-comments.png) · [sent](img/page-1400-dark-7b-comments-sent.png): beside the wall, never over it — except on a page narrower than 720 px (beside an open rail), where the drawers lie over the wall's right half and **Fit** fills the part left uncovered (the whole wall when less than 240 px is left). Tabs Open / Sent / Resolved with counts, comments grouped by screen with their pin number, device size and element; click one to jump to it; ✓ resolves. The footer button says what it does — **Send 3 comments to chat** — and when it cannot, says why. Sending makes one revision request that also says which pinned elements moved or are missing; it goes to the linked chat (or, when that chat isn't selected, opens it and is delivered there; or starts a new chat). If the agent is busy it waits in the chat box. If the chat got the message but the comments could not be marked as sent, a notice says to resolve them by hand (they are never reported as a failed send).

**Versions drawer (P4)** — [compare](img/page-1400-dark-8-versions-compare.png): newest first with the reason ("After agent turn", "Auto-saved", "Saved manually", "Before restore", "Restored"), a **Current** mark or an **Unsaved changes** row. **View** shows that version on the wall (read only, with a bar: Compare with current · Restore · Back to current); **Compare** splits each artboard — the version on the left, the current design on the right — and lists the changed files; **Restore** says the current state is saved first.

**Settings (P5)** — [sheet](img/page-1400-dark-13-settings.png): the same content as the [rail's settings](rail-panel.md) (folder, earlier folders, help your agent find your designs, browser, opencode.json), in a side sheet.

**Page viewer (P6)** — [full width](img/page-1440-dark-14-page-full-width.jpg) · [laptop view with pins](img/page-1440-dark-15-page-laptop-pins.jpg) · [export menu](img/page-1400-dark-11-artifact-export-menu.png) · [exported](img/page-1400-dark-12-artifact-exported.png)
- A page is shown like a browser tab, not on a wall: there is no pan and no zoom, and no zoom controls. Toolbar: back, title, **View**, **Comment**, then **Comments · n**, **Versions**, **Export ▾** (Download ZIP, Download single-file HTML), **Copy ▾** (Copy HTML when it fits the clipboard limit, Copy path), **Continue in chat**, ⋯.
- **View:** **Full width** (the default) fills the viewer and the page scrolls inside its own frame. Each design size ("Laptop · 1280×800") shows the page at that width, centred, as tall as the viewer, shrunk to fit when the viewer is narrower and never enlarged. The choice is remembered per design; a size the agent removes falls back to Full width.
- The page is interactive like a browser. **Comment** turns on comment mode: hover outlines elements, a click opens the composer. Pins work in every view, Full width included: every comment on the page shows in every view, re-found in the current layout, and a new comment remembers the view it was made in ("Made at full width" in its card). Pins that would overlap sit side by side.
- Wheel or trackpad over the page scrolls it; arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Home and End scroll it when the viewer has focus. Ctrl/⌘ + wheel, **+ / −**, **Shift+0 / Shift+1**, Space-drag and **H** do nothing here.
- **Remote files:** when the page loads files from the internet, a warning says "This page loads N files from the internet" and "They're blocked here because designs run offline:", then lists up to 5 of them by host and path (the full address on hover) and "+ n more". **Ask the agent to fix it** asks the service for an offline request that lists every blocked address and puts it in the linked chat's message box (opening that chat, or starting one); you send it. The button spins while that runs; a failure shows the service's sentence. **Hide** puts the banner away for that design until OpenChamber restarts or the extension reloads (closing and reopening the design keeps it hidden); it comes back if a file it didn't list gets blocked, and stays away if some of the listed ones are fixed. The comments drawer note and the revision's URL list are not affected. The same banner appears on a canvas ("This design loads…"), whose artboards also keep their "N remote" badges. With blocked files, the comments drawer says "The blocked files are listed in the request too." The banner is hidden while you look at an old version.
- After an export the toast carries the path and Copy, and a banner offers **Show in folder**.

## Behaviour by role
One role: the OpenChamber user. "Continue in chat" reopens the linked chat, or starts a new one. A design chip or "Open as page" that targets this page opens that design.

## Data
**Reads:** as the [rail](rail-panel.md), plus renders of each screen/viewport and version (`GET /designs/:slug/render`).
**Writes:** comments, rename, delete (to `.trash`), versions (snapshot, restore), exports (to Downloads), settings, loose-file import (`POST /loose/import`), the OpenCode skill (`POST /agent/skill`, only from its button). Per-design view settings (hidden sizes and camera for a canvas, `pageView` for a page) are saved with `host.storage` under `view:<slug>`. A comment made on a page records its view in `viewport` (`fill` for Full width). "Ask the agent to fix it" calls `POST /designs/:slug/prompt` with `purpose: 'offline'`.

## States & edge cases
- Every data view has loading (skeletons / scoped spinners), empty (says what goes here and how to add it), error (the service's sentence + Try again, input kept) and success states.
- A design deleted while open shows "This design is gone" with a way back; a pending design shows "isn't written yet" boards that fill in live ([screenshot](img/page-1400-dark-live-1-not-written.png)).
- Remote assets are blocked by the host; the artboard's "N remote" badge and the remote-files banner say so. When nothing is blocked any more, "Ask the agent to fix it" says "This design doesn't load anything from the internet."
- A page's frame follows the viewer's size (resizes settle for 100 ms) without reloading; a drawer laid over a narrow page makes the frame narrower instead of covering it.
- The page never puts agent-written HTML in its own DOM: every artboard is a sandboxed frame (`allow-scripts`, never `allow-same-origin`).
